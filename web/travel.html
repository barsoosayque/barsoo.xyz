Travel

Places I've been to and lived in.

🇷🇺 Russia

Born and raised. Have been to both capitals, and a bunch of Siberia. Haven't really explored the motherland that much to be honest, which is a shame. I would really like to explore Karelia and Sakhalin.

-2024, 2026

🇲🇪 Montenegro

A true Balkan gem. Have nothing but great memories from my 1 year spent in Podgorica and few other cities. Phenomenal nature and absolute chads living in there. Was surprised to find a lot of locals to speak broken russian from the days of socialist days.

2024, 2025

🇹🇭 Thailand

Was living there for 4 months in Autumn-Winter. Felt myself really small and lost in the concrete mazes of Bangkok. The public transportation was pretty nice, but it was incresingly depressing for me to stay there. The food is sick though ! But I wasn't able to embrace local culture besides that...

2025, 2026

🇻🇳 Vietnam

Spent 4 months in Spring-Summer in Da Nang. What a great place, had lots of fun interactions there. I vividly remember relaxed evenings along the seaside, and swimming at night (even though it was probably illegal). Had absolute blast eating at somebody's kitchen which they repurpused to serve Pho.
